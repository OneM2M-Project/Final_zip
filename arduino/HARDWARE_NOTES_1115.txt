X-Guard hardware team -> software team (2026-11-15)
We changed the three files below to match our rig. Just copy them over the old ones at the same paths.

1) xguard_2sensor/xguard_2sensor.ino  (Arduino code; this is the only sketch you need)
   - SG90 servo, powered straight from the Arduino 5V/GND (no separate adapter)
   - The servo only gets a signal while pushing and is detached once it's back (less load on USB power, less jitter)
   - No e-stop button (USE_ESTOP=false). Stop the line with the STOP command
   - Pins (D2 entry / D3 before the rejector / D9 servo) and the serial format (DET/OK/NG/REJ/PASS) are unchanged
   - For PlatformIO, paste this file into arduino_2sensor/src/main.cpp as is (same content)

2) gateway/image_source.py  (camera)
   - Opens the ELP camera with MSMF (with DSHOW the picture is completely black)
   - Rotates the image 180 degrees (the camera is mounted upside down, and the training photos were taken rotated 180 too)
   - About 3 s after start it prints "카메라 확인: MSMF 1280x800, 밝기 ..." and warns if the image is black or the resolution is off
   - The camera shoots the belt side-on from one side, with the light on the opposite side (850 nm, 12 V)

3) env.example  (new camera settings)
   Please add these to the .env on the demo PC too (use that PC's COM port):
     SERIAL_PORT=COM4
     IMAGE_SOURCE=camera
     CAMERA_INDEX=1
     CAMERA_BACKEND=MSMF
     CAMERA_FOURCC=MJPG
     CAMERA_ROTATE=180
     CAPTURE_DELAY_MS=0   (when the entry sensor S1 is directly in front of the camera; if it's upstream, the travel time in ms)
